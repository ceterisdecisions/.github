<h1 align="center">Ceteris Decisions</h1>

<p align="center"><b>Measure the effect. Understand the context. Decide with judgment.</b></p>

Models assume *ceteris paribus*: all else equal. Reality never holds still.
We help marketing teams isolate the real effect of their investment, understand everything else that moves around it, and keep people in charge of the decision.

### What we build

- **Ready** · [`mmm_ready`](https://github.com/ceterisdecisions/mmm_ready): open-source dbt package that turns marketing data into MMM-ready tables and tells you whether your data is fit for modeling.
- **Context** · curated control data (calendar, weather, macro) delivered where your data lives: Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery. *Coming soon.*
- **Measure** · marketing mix modeling and incrementality kits. *Coming soon.*
- **Decide** · AI agents that inform decisions, with humans making the call. *Coming soon.*

### Principles

- **AI informs, humans decide.**
- **Data quality before modeling.** Most MMMs fail on inputs, not on math.
- **Platform-agnostic.** dbt plus open models: Meridian, PyMC-Marketing, Robyn.
